Privacy Policy

Effective November 13, 2026 · applies to the Kawa Friends app and kawafriends.com

Kawa Friends works without an account. We do not collect, sell, or share personal information for advertising, and the app contains no advertising or cross-app tracking SDKs.

What you write stays yours

Your to-dos, goals, everyday things and birthday are kept on your device and, when you are signed in to iCloud, in your own iCloud storage so they carry across your iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch. That storage belongs to your Apple Account — we cannot read it.

Anything you write on a piece of paper at the campfire is burned: it is never saved and never leaves your device.

Friends in their own words (optional)

The friends speak lines written in the app. If you choose to let them answer in their own words, the app sends the title of the to-do or goal they are talking about and the friend's name to our server, which asks Anthropic's Claude for one short line and passes it back. Nothing else is sent, there is no account or message history, and we do not keep what was sent. You are asked before anything is sent, and you can turn it off at any time in the calendar panel.

What we collect

To fix bugs and see which parts of the app are used, it sends a small number of anonymous usage events — counts such as "a to-do was ticked", never what was written — tagged with a random identifier made on install, the app version and the OS version. This identifier stays on the device, is not linked to you or your Apple Account, is not used for tracking or advertising, and matches the App Store privacy label.

If you share analytics with app developers in your device's settings, the app also sends the crash and performance reports Apple's MetricKit gives it — with the app version and device model, not tied to that identifier.

Feedback

If you write to us from the envelope row in the app, the message is kept for up to 90 days so we can read it and reply inside the app. It is tagged only with the same random identifier — no email or name.

Notifications

Reminders for to-dos with a time are scheduled on your device. They are not sent through our servers.

This website

kawafriends.com counts anonymous page visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics. No cookies, no accounts.

Children

Kawa Friends is for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

We may update this policy as the app grows; material changes appear here with a new effective date.
