Kawa Friends

Help

Your to-dos, kept with five friends in a small village

Where are my to-dos?

On the board in the village square. Tap the date at the top of the screen to read it; tap a note to tick it.

How do I add one?

While reading the board, tap a suggestion to pin it, or write a line at the bottom. The flag makes it a goal; the clock gives it a time.

Why did a friend walk over?

To remind you of something on the board, or to offer to pin something you did before. Walk on and nothing changes.

Reminders at a time

Give a to-do a time and you get one notification at that time, with a Done button. Turn notifications off in the iPhone's Settings ▸ Kawa Friends.

Widget and Apple Watch

Add the Kawa Friends widget to the Home Screen, or open the app on your Apple Watch, to see today's to-dos and tick them there.

A new iPhone

Sign in to the same iCloud account and your board comes with you.

Friends answering in their own words

Optional, and off until you say yes. Turn it on or off in the calendar panel on the board. See Privacy for what is sent.

Write to us

In the app: read the board ▸ the calendar button ▸ the envelope row. We reply there. Or email hello@swiftian.com.
